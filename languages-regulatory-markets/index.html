Which languages does Latin Link specialise in?

Our strongest medical teams translate into English, Spanish (European and Latin American) and Portuguese (European and Brazilian). FAQ

Languages and markets: common questions

Can’t see your question? Email projects@latinlink.com or call +44 (0)20 3286 4122.

Do you localise Spanish for each Latin American country?

Yes. We decide the target country before translation starts and use native linguists and country-specific glossaries, rather than a single generic “LATAM Spanish”.

Do you translate for both Brazil and Portugal?

Yes, with separate native teams. Brazilian Portuguese and European Portuguese are treated as different target languages.

Which regulators do you prepare documentation for?

We translate documentation for submissions and correspondence with the EMA, MHRA, FDA, ANVISA, COFEPRIS, INVIMA, ANMAT, ISP Chile and DIGEMID, among others.

Can you handle Asian languages?

Yes. Alongside our core European and Latin American teams, we cover Asian languages as projects need, with the same translation and independent revision workflow.

Can one team manage a multi-country study?

Yes. A dedicated project management team coordinates every language, with shared glossaries and translation memories for consistent terminology across countries.
