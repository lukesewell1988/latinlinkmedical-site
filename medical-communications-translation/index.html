What is medical communications translation?

Medical communications translation covers the scientific and educational content written about medicines and medical science, including journal manuscripts and abstracts, medical education, medical brochures and presentations. Much of it is translated into English for publication, where the text must read as if it had been written by a native scientific author.

FAQ

Medical communications: common questions

More answers on our main FAQ.

Can you translate a scientific paper into English for publication?

Yes. We translate your manuscript into English, help optimise it for the guidelines of UK journals and can advise on its fit for the publication you are targeting.

Which therapy areas do you cover for publications?

We regularly translate manuscripts in oncology, cardiovascular, dermatology, general medicine, mental health, neurology, pain management and women’s health.

Who are your scientific translators?

Our scientific translators have a full scientific background, with a minimum of a master’s degree or PhD in a scientific discipline, and translate only within their field.
