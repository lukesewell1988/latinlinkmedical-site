Who is Latin Link?

Latin Link is a trading name of Language Partners Ltd, a company based in London, United Kingdom. Founded by linguists and scientists, our team has specialised in medical and clinical translation for over 15 years, working for pharmaceutical and biotech sponsors, CROs, clinical research sites, medical device companies and medical publishers.

Governance

Governance and accountability

Clear responsibilities, independent checks and documented quality, so your quality and procurement teams can rely on how we work.

  • Named accountability

    Every client has a dedicated account manager and project management team, so you always know who is responsible for your work.

  • Independent checks

    No linguist revises their own translation. Revision, back-translation and reconciliation are carried out by different, independent linguists.

  • Controlled linguist selection

    Linguists are selected for their language pair and subject, with clinical experience and at least 5 years’ translation experience, and reviewed on every project.

  • Data protection

    Personal data is handled under UK GDPR, with appropriate safeguards when linguists outside the UK work on a project. See our privacy notice.

  • Documented quality

    A workflow aligned with ISO 17100, versioned records, reconciliation reports and signed Certificates of Translation Accuracy on request.

  • Reliable delivery

    Teams across several time zones and a clear delivery plan agreed with you at the start of every project.
