We are Latin Link

Translation can only be perfected through experience

Latin Link is a specialist translation company founded by a group of linguists and scientists, dedicated to an outstanding, tailored service for the medical, life-science, scientific and technical sectors.

We are based in London, with production teams across Europe and Latin America working in several time zones. Founded on our expertise in Latin America, we now translate into all major European languages, from one-off projects to highly specialised scientific texts. We do not claim to translate every language pair in the world: we focus on the ones we do best, because every language has dialects that need an expert’s care.

  • Established, hand-picked team

    Every translator is selected for their language pair and subject, and we value and keep a trusted, reliable translator base.

  • Perfected through experience

    Linguists with at least five years’ translation experience and real work experience in a pharmaceutical, clinical or scientific environment.

  • Only into their knowledge area

    Specialists translate only within their field and only into their native language, so the science is understood, not just the words.

  • No-nonsense about deadlines

    A reliable attitude to deadlines, with teams across several time zones and same-day jobs available.

How we ensure quality

Our translation process: five steps, two linguists, every time

We delegate each project carefully, then manage it through self-review and independent review at every stage. No translation reaches you without being checked by a second specialist.

  1. Step 01

    Free, customised quote

    Send your files through our client portal or by email. We reply with a fast quote built around your requirements, for one-off projects and long-term partnerships alike.

  2. Step 02

    Dedicated account manager

    Once confirmed, a dedicated tri-lingual account manager is your point of contact, and the project is delegated to the right linguist for the language and subject.

  3. Step 03

    Native specialist translation

    A native linguist who works only within their field translates into their mother tongue, using CAT tools, translation memories and your glossaries.

  4. Step 04

    Independent review and QA

    A second linguist reviews the translation against the source, followed by scientific, statistical, editorial and quality control checks.

  5. Step 05

    Delivery on the agreed deadline

    Your project manager returns the translation in your required format, on the deadline we agreed.

  • Two linguists on every project

    A specialist translator and a second reviewing linguist, as standard, never as an add-on.

  • Hand-picked, qualified specialists

    At least 5 years’ translation experience, work experience in a pharmaceutical or clinical setting, and qualifications in linguistics and a scientific discipline.

  • Terminology you can rely on

    Translation memories and glossaries in Trados and memoQ for consistent terminology across documents.

  • Confidential handling

    Files are shared only with the team working on your project. We are happy to work under your NDA.

What is medical translation?

Medical translation is the translation of clinical, regulatory, pharmaceutical, medical device and healthcare documents by linguists who understand the science behind them. Errors can affect patients, approvals and trial results, so Latin Link uses native specialists with scientific qualifications and a second reviewing linguist on every project.

What is clinical trial translation?

Clinical trial translation is the translation of study documents such as protocols, informed consent forms, Investigator’s Brochures, Quality of Life instruments and clinical study reports, so that sponsors, sites, ethics committees and patients in each country receive an exact, consistent version in their own language.

Which Latin American regulators do you translate documentation for?

We translate documentation for submissions to ANVISA (Brazil), COFEPRIS (Mexico), INVIMA (Colombia), DIGEMID (Peru) and ANMAT (Argentina), in Brazilian Portuguese and the Spanish used in each country.

Which languages do you translate?

Our strongest medical teams translate into English, Spanish (European and Latin American) and Portuguese (European and Brazilian). We also have strong French and Italian teams, and cover all European and Asian languages as projects need. Our translators work only into their native language.

Who are your translators?

Native specialists with at least five years’ translation experience and significant work experience in a pharmaceutical or clinical environment. We require qualifications in linguistics and in a scientific discipline; our scientific translators hold a master’s degree or PhD. They translate only within their knowledge area.

Do two linguists really work on every project?

Yes. Every project is translated by a native specialist and then reviewed by a second qualified linguist, followed by scientific, statistical, editorial and quality control checks before delivery. It is our standard service, not an add-on.

How does your process relate to ISO 17100?

ISO 17100 is the international standard for translation services. Our workflow is aligned with ISO 17100: qualified linguists, revision by a second linguist, managed projects and verification before delivery.

Do you offer machine translation post-editing (MTPE)?

Yes. In MTPE a CAT tool first machine-translates the document using a translation memory, then a specialist translator checks and edits every line, and our editors check it again. It suits large documents with tight deadlines.

Can you localise Spanish for different countries?

Yes. Our native linguists translate into Spanish for Spain and localise for specific Latin American countries, taking account of each country’s idioms and terminology.

Can you handle urgent or high-volume work?

Yes. Same-day jobs are available, and our teams across several time zones let us scale for large, multi-language projects. Tell us your deadline when you request a quote.

Which tools and file formats do you work with?

We are experts in Trados and memoQ and use translation memories and glossaries for consistent terminology. We adapt to your preferred CAT tool and have desktop publishing software for print-ready files.

How do you keep our documents confidential?

Client documents are shared only with the team working on your project. We are happy to work under your NDA.

How do I get a quote?

Use the quote form, email the Latin Link Projects Team or send files through our client portal. We provide free, fast, customised quotes for one-off projects and longer-term partnerships alike.
