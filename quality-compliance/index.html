Quality & compliance

Medical translation that stands up to audit. Every document is translated by a native medical linguist and independently revised by a second linguist, in a workflow aligned with ISO 17100, with back-translation and signed Certificates of Translation Accuracy when you need them.

What does an ISO 17100-aligned workflow mean?

ISO 17100 is the international standard for translation services. Latin Link’s workflow is aligned with ISO 17100: qualified native linguists translate, a second qualified linguist independently revises every document against the source, a dedicated project management team manages each project, and final verification checks completeness, consistency and formatting before delivery. It describes how we work; it is not a certification claim.

Workflow

Translation first, then independent and specialist review

The same five-stage workflow runs on every project, from a single consent form to a multi-country submission.

  1. Step 01

    Scoping and preparation

    Your project manager confirms source versions, target countries, reference material and the deadline, and briefs the team.

  2. Step 02

    Specialist translation

    A native medical linguist with clinical experience translates into their mother tongue, using your glossary and the programme translation memory.

  3. Step 03

    Independent revision

    A second qualified linguist compares the translation against the source for accuracy, terminology, completeness and style.

  4. Step 04

    Specialist review

    Where needed, a clinical or in-country specialist reviews patient-facing and highly technical content for usability and local practice.

  5. Step 05

    Verification and delivery

    Final QA on numbers, units, cross-references and formatting, then delivery with a signed Certificate of Translation Accuracy on request.

Safeguards

What protects your documents

  • Clinical and medical linguists

    Native medical linguists with clinical experience and at least 5 years’ translation experience, who translate only within their field.

  • Back-translation and reconciliation

    For ICFs, patient materials and clinical outcome assessments, an independent back-translation and a documented reconciliation of every discrepancy.

  • Signed Certificates of Translation Accuracy

    Signed certificates that identify the document, its version and the language pair, ready for sites, ethics committees and your trial master file.

  • Confidentiality and secure handling

    Files are shared only with the linguists and project managers working on your project, through access-controlled systems. We are happy to work under your NDA.

  • Terminology control

    Approved glossaries and translation memories in Trados and memoQ keep terminology consistent across documents, countries and amendments.

  • Version control for amendments

    A register of documents, versions and languages for each programme, so amendments flow through every dependent translation.

FAQ

Quality and compliance: common questions

Can’t see your question? Email projects@latinlink.com or call +44 (0)20 3286 4122.

How does your workflow relate to ISO 17100?

We describe our workflow as aligned with ISO 17100. It follows the standard’s requirements for linguist competence, revision by a second linguist, project management and verification before delivery.

Do two linguists work on every document?

Yes. Every document is translated by a native specialist and independently revised by a second qualified linguist. This is standard, not an add-on.

Can you provide Certificates of Translation Accuracy?

Yes. We provide signed Certificates of Translation Accuracy on request, identifying the document, version, language pair and date.

Do you offer back-translation?

Yes. For patient-facing materials and clinical outcome assessments we can arrange an independent back-translation and a documented reconciliation report.

How do you protect confidential documents?

Client files are shared only with the team working on your project, on access-controlled systems, and we are happy to work under your NDA. See our privacy notice for how personal data is handled.
